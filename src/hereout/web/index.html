                About HereOut

                • A plan you can't read

                  Abroad, the evacuation plan by the door may be in a language you can't read. HereOut turns it into a route with steps in your language.

                • Muse Spark: exits and plan words

                  Meta's model looks for every exit, looks again at doubtful ones on zoomed crops, and reads the plan's words. It can translate them into your language: 12 besides English.

                • Accuracy

                  On 28 test photos that no exit rule was tuned on, HereOut found about half of the exits, and about half of the exits it marked were wrong. So check every route against the plan, and mark the exit yourself when it is wrong. How it was measured

                Built solo by devrives for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon, with Muse Spark through Baseten, Python, OpenCV and plain JavaScript. Code on GitLab · Photo credits
